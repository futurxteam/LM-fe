Privacy Policy

Futureace Healthcare Academy — Lead Management CRM (sale.futureaceacademy.com)

Futureace Healthcare Academy ("Futureace Academy", "we", "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you share with us through our website, admissions forms, and Meta (Facebook and Instagram) Lead Ads.

1. Information We Collect

When you submit an admissions inquiry or lead form, we may collect:

  • Full name, phone number, and email address
  • Educational qualification, preferred study location, and course of interest
  • Campaign, form, and submission timestamp metadata provided via Meta Lead Ads

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To contact you regarding course details, eligibility, fee structure, and admissions counseling
  • To assign your inquiry to an authorized Futureace Academy admissions counselor
  • To maintain internal admissions records and improve student support

3. Data Sharing & Security

We do not sell or rent your personal data to third parties. Access to candidate lead data inside sale.futureaceacademy.com is restricted to authorized Futureace Academy staff and admissions counselors.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time. See our Data Deletion Instructions or email us at info@futureaceacademy.com.
