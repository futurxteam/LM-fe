User Data Deletion Instructions

Futureace Healthcare Academy — Lead Management CRM (sales.futureaceacademy.com)

If you have submitted an inquiry through our website or Meta (Facebook / Instagram) Lead Ads and would like your personal data permanently removed from our CRM database, please follow the steps below.

How to Request Deletion

  1. Send an email to info@futureaceacademy.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request".
  2. Include your full name and the phone number or email address you used when submitting the form so we can locate your record.

Processing Timeline

Upon verifying your request, our admissions operations team will permanently delete your lead record and associated contact logs from sales.futureaceacademy.com within 7 business days and send you a confirmation email.

For full details on how we handle personal information, please review our Privacy Policy.
